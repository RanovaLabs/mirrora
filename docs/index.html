Your iPhone on the big screen.

Mirrora turns your Android TV or Google TV into a screen-mirroring receiver for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Pick your TV in Control Center and your screen, photos, videos and sound appear on the TV. No cables, no account, no ads.

Get it on Google Play  ·  Source code on GitHub

Works with what you have

Uses the Screen Mirroring button already in Control Center on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Nothing to install on your Apple device.

Picture and sound

Hardware video decoding for smooth mirroring that follows rotation, plus sound, including music played straight to the TV.

Always ready

Starts with your TV and stays discoverable in the background. Mirroring opens full screen on its own and closes when you stop.

Private by design

Everything stays on your home network. Mirrora has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and never records what you show.

How to use it

  1. Install Mirrora on your Android TV or Google TV and open it once.
  2. Choose Allow so Mirrora can open by itself when you start mirroring.
  3. On your iPhone, iPad or Mac, open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and pick your TV.

Your Apple device and TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. Having trouble? See Support.

Free and open source

Mirrora is free software, licensed under the GNU GPL v3. It builds on the open-source java-airplay project. The full source is on GitHub.
