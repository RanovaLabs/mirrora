Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

The short version. Mirrora has no server and no accounts. We do not collect, receive or store any of your personal data. What you mirror goes directly from your Apple device to your TV over your home network, is shown live, and is never recorded or sent anywhere else.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Mirrora Android TV app ("the app") published by Ranova Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17500622, registered office 82a James Carter Road, Mildenhall, Bury St. Edmunds, England, IP28 7DE) ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Nothing about you or your use of the app is sent to us.

How the app uses your network

  • Being found. So that iPhones, iPads and Macs can find your TV, the app announces itself on your local network using Bonjour (mDNS). The announcement contains your TV's device name, its local network address and a random identifier created by the app. It is only visible to devices on your own network.
  • Receiving. When you choose your TV in Screen Mirroring, your Apple device sends your screen, audio and media to the TV over your local network. The app decodes it and shows or plays it straight away. It is not recorded, saved or forwarded.
  • Online video. If a device on your network asks the app to play a video from the internet, the TV fetches that video directly from the address it was given. We are not involved and receive nothing.

What the app stores on your TV

One item: the random identifier mentioned above, so your Apple devices recognise the TV from one day to the next. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. Like any Android app, Mirrora may write technical messages to the TV's system log, which stays on the TV.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

Network access, Wi-Fi state and multicast
To be discoverable on your local network and receive the streams you send.
Run in the background and start at boot
So your TV is always ready to receive, without opening the app first. Android shows a notification while this is running.
Display over other apps
So the app can bring mirroring to the screen when you start it, whatever app is open. It is used for nothing else.
Keep awake
Only while you are mirroring or playing, so the picture and sound don't stop.

Children

Mirrora is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, disclose, sell or delete. We have never sold user data and have none to sell.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date changed before that version is released.
